Sola Shorts Bot

Sola Shorts Bot is a personal tool used only by the artist Sola Delvaux to upload her own original music Shorts to her own YouTube channel.

Privacy Policy

This app uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data from other users. The app is not offered to the public.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
